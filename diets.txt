Here’s a breakdown of 20 super common diets, along with what each one typically emphasizes, includes, and avoids:

⸻

1. Keto (Ketogenic)
	•	Eats: High fat, moderate protein, very low carbs (e.g., meats, cheese, eggs, avocados, oils)
	•	Avoids: Bread, pasta, sugar, most fruits, grains

⸻

2. Paleo
	•	Eats: Whole foods like meat, fish, eggs, fruits, veggies, nuts, seeds
	•	Avoids: Grains, legumes, dairy, refined sugar, processed foods

⸻

3. Vegan
	•	Eats: Plants only—fruits, vegetables, legumes, grains, nuts, seeds
	•	Avoids: All animal products (meat, dairy, eggs, honey)

⸻

4. Vegetarian
	•	Eats: Plant-based foods, dairy, and eggs
	•	Avoids: Meat, fish, poultry

⸻

5. Mediterranean
	•	Eats: Fruits, vegetables, whole grains, fish, olive oil, nuts, legumes
	•	Avoids: Processed foods, red meat (in excess), added sugars

⸻

6. DASH (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension)
	•	Eats: Vegetables, fruits, low-fat dairy, whole grains, lean proteins
	•	Avoids: High-sodium foods, sugary beverages, red meats

⸻

7. Whole30
	•	Eats: Whole foods—meat, seafood, veggies, fruits, nuts
	•	Avoids: Sugar, grains, dairy, legumes, alcohol, processed foods (for 30 days)

⸻

8. Low-Carb
	•	Eats: Protein, fat, low-carb vegetables
	•	Avoids: Sugar, bread, pasta, most fruits and grains

⸻

9. Carnivore
	•	Eats: Only animal products—meat, fish, eggs
	•	Avoids: All plant foods, carbs, sugar

⸻

10. Raw Food
	•	Eats: Uncooked, unprocessed foods—raw fruits, veggies, nuts, seeds
	•	Avoids: Cooked foods, processed foods, animal products (sometimes)

⸻

11. Flexitarian
	•	Eats: Mostly plant-based, occasional meat/fish
	•	Avoids: No strict restrictions—minimizes processed and red meats

⸻

12. Intermittent Fasting (IF)
	•	Eats: Normal foods during eating windows (depends on method)
	•	Avoids: Eating outside designated time windows (e.g., 16:8, 5:2)

⸻

13. Gluten-Free
	•	Eats: Naturally gluten-free foods—fruits, vegetables, rice, meat
	•	Avoids: Wheat, barley, rye, most breads/pastas

⸻

14. Zone Diet
	•	Eats: Balanced ratio (40% carbs, 30% protein, 30% fat)
	•	Avoids: Refined carbs, sugars, processed foods

⸻

15. South Beach
	•	Eats: Lean protein, low-GI carbs, healthy fats
	•	Avoids: High-GI carbs, sugar, white bread, potatoes

⸻

16. Atkins
	•	Eats: High-protein, high-fat, low-carb foods
	•	Avoids: Sugar, grains, high-carb veggies/fruits (phased reintroduction)

⸻

17. Ayurvedic Diet
	•	Eats: Tailored to body type (dosha)—often whole, warm, seasonal foods
	•	Avoids: Processed foods, incompatible food pairings, “cold” or heavy foods (based on dosha)

⸻

18. Pescatarian
	•	Eats: Vegetarian diet plus fish/seafood
	•	Avoids: Red meat, poultry

⸻

19. Low-FODMAP
	•	Eats: Low-FODMAP fruits, veggies, gluten-free grains, lactose-free dairy
	•	Avoids: Fermentable carbs (like onions, garlic, beans, wheat, dairy)

⸻

20. Macro Counting (IIFYM - If It Fits Your Macros)
	•	Eats: Anything, as long as it fits daily targets for protein, carbs, fat
	•	Avoids: No specific foods—focus is on quantities/macronutrient tracking

⸻
